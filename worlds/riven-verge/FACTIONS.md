# Factions

Long-lived organisations, their standing and their leadership. **No ruleset's numbers** —
not a faction's level, not its soldiers' stat blocks, not its treasury in gold.

## The Office of the Count

**Low Shoal. Older than every government that has tried to own it.**

Keeps the Reckoning: the day, the span, the year, and the position of the drift. It has no
army, no territory and no revenue beyond fees, and it is the single most obeyed institution
in the Verge, because every contract, every crossing and every claim is dated and a date
means nothing without them.

The Office has survived by never once taking a side and never once being wrong. It is
currently led by a person whose name is not used in official documents — the Count signs as
the Count — and the succession is internal and not explained to anyone.

**What they want:** the count unbroken. That is the whole of it, and it makes them
predictable in a way that is useful to everybody and exploitable by anyone patient.

## The Thrennet houses

**Thrennet. Perhaps forty years of real power, and a much older pretence of it.**

Not one faction but a cartel that behaves as one when crossings are threatened. They own
most of the means of getting between shards and price them by the span. In Touching they are
merchants; in Falling they are the only reason anybody is anywhere they chose to be.

**What they want:** the crossings scarce enough to be worth money and reliable enough to be
worth buying. A cheap crossing is as much a threat to them as no crossing at all.

## The Shoreward Watch

**Every shard, loosely. Nobody's authority but their own.**

People who go over the shore on ropes. They salvage, survey, and bring back what falls
off — and they are the only body with practical knowledge of the underside of a shard,
which is a kind of power nobody has yet worked out how to use.

**What they want:** to be left alone and paid. They are not an organisation so much as a
trade with a shared vocabulary and a grudge against the Thrennet houses.

## The house records

**Not a faction. Listed here because people act on it.**

Several of the oldest families on the outer shards keep, in their house records, a
**direction** — inherited, maintained, and pointing at nothing anyone can see. They came
from the arrivals (`HISTORY.md`, 140–180 VR) and the direction is said to be the way home.

They are not organised and they do not agree with each other. What they share is that any of
them would fund an expedition, immediately and badly, for anyone who could make the
direction mean something.

**What they want:** the one thing nobody in the Verge has ever managed. This is the hook the
world is built around; use it sparingly.
