# {{MAP_NAME}}

An ASCII tactical grid. **One square is 5 feet.** Maintain one for any fight where position
matters — flanking, cover, reach, area templates, difficult terrain — and update token
positions each round.

Columns are letters, rows are numbers, so a square is named like `C4`.

```
     A    B    C    D    E    F    G    H
  1  #    #    #    #    #    #    #    #
  2  #    .    .    .    .    .    .    #
  3  #    .    ~    ~    .    .    .    #
  4  #    .    ~    K    .    .    o    #
  5  #    .    .    .    .    g    .    #
  6  #    .    .    .    .    g    .    #
  7  #    +    #    #    #    #    #    #
```

## Terrain key

| Symbol | Means | Mechanical effect |
|---|---|---|
| `#` | wall | blocks movement and line of effect |
| `.` | open floor | — |
| `~` | rising water / rubble | difficult terrain: each square costs 10 feet |
| `^` | deep difficult terrain | each square costs 15 feet |
| `+` | door | closed unless noted |
| `o` | object / lever / cover | provides standard cover (+2 AC) when used |
| `=` | low wall | lesser cover (+1 AC) |
| `:` | hazard | name it and cite its source |
