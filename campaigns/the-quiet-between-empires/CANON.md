# Canon — The Quiet Between Empires

**Append-only.** Established facts that must never be contradicted. A fact lands here the
moment it is said out loud in play: a name, a date, a relationship, a geographic claim, a
price, a rule of the world.

The GM never contradicts this file. If a contradiction is needed, the GM asks the player for
a retcon instead — and a retcon is recorded as a new line saying what changed, not as an
edit to the old one.

Facts the player knows are unmarked. Facts established on the GM's side that the player's
characters have not learned are marked **GM-side truth**, so they cannot later be
contradicted but also are not treated as something the character has heard.
