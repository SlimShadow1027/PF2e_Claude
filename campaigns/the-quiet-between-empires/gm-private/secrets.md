## The thing under the fen

> **GM-ONLY.** Never printed into chat unless the player asks for a spoiler.

Established by the player at intake, so it is theirs, not a GM invention:

- An **ancient evil was sealed in a ruin** in or beneath this region.
- **Dwarven excavation unearthed it** at the tail of the last great interhuman war — a few
  hundred years before play begins.
- Once loose, **it toppled a city.**
- **Later histories recorded that city's fall as a casualty of the war.** That is the version in
  every record a player character could consult. It is wrong, and it is the only version written
  down.
- **Who knows otherwise:** a handful of dwarves, a couple of elves, and almost nobody else. They
  are of the generation that is now mostly dead. The truth has an expiry date measured in
  funerals.
- **Now:** it directs the goblin bands using the surfacing causeways. The goblins are organised
  far past their own capacity, which is the visible symptom a player can notice early.

### Resolved — the entity is the author

Answered by the player: **the entity is deliberately reshaping the underground**, not exploiting a
natural process. There is no second, will-less cause. The shifting began deep and is being pushed
upward on purpose, toward the mine-heads and the holdings above.

What that buys the campaign: one causal chain and one thing to stop. What it costs: the original
premise's "no will behind it" theme is gone, and with it the idea that the campaign has no
antagonist at its centre. It has one, and it is underground.

### Setting relocation

The fen is out. This is mountain and mine country — a **Daedalus labyrinth** that will not hold its
shape, reached through dwarven workings that broke into a Thassilonian ruin. Nothing of the
Mushfens framing survives: no low water, no thaw, no causeways.

## Encounter notes — upper workings watch post (session 1)

- XP: Goblin Commando (lvl 1, party+0 = 40) + Goblin Warrior (lvl −1, party−2 = 20) = **60 XP, severe** for party level 1 of 2 (`pf2e.py encounter`).
- **If the alarm cord is pulled:** it rattles bones and tin down the side passage to the band's den. **Three rounds later**, 2 more Goblin Warriors (Monster Core p.174) arrive from the side passage. For the rest of this delve the upper workings are on alert: sentries are doubled and Stealth is contested at Perception +2 (an alert-state ruling, GM call).
- If a goblin flees down the side passage, the same result follows on a 2-round delay.
- Commando tactic: reach 10 ft and trips with the horsechopper. It hangs back by the fire and lets the warrior run.
