## The thing under the fen

> **GM-ONLY.** Never printed into chat unless the player asks for a spoiler.

Established by the player at intake, so it is theirs, not a GM invention:

- An **ancient evil was sealed in a ruin** in or beneath this region.
- **Dwarven excavation unearthed it** at the tail of the last great interhuman war — a few
  hundred years before play begins.
- Once loose, **it toppled a city.**
- **Later histories recorded that city's fall as a casualty of the war.** That is the version in
  every record a player character could consult. It is wrong, and it is the only version written
  down.
- **Who knows otherwise:** a handful of dwarves, a couple of elves, and almost nobody else. They
  are of the generation that is now mostly dead. The truth has an expiry date measured in
  funerals.
- **Now:** it directs the goblin bands using the surfacing causeways. The goblins are organised
  far past their own capacity, which is the visible symptom a player can notice early.

### Open question — needs the player's answer before session 1

**Is the road network surfacing because of this being, or independently of it?**

- *Because of it* — the being is reactivating the network, giving the campaign one causal chain
  and one thing to stop.
- *Independently* — the low water and the returning magic expose the causeways, and the being is
  simply the first intelligence to understand what a road is for. Keeps the premise's original
  "no will behind it" theme and makes the being an opportunist rather than an author.
