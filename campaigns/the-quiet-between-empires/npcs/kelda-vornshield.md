# Kelda Vornshield — the Last Witness

- **Role:** GM-run sidekick, the protagonist's employer. One job: *knowing*. Lore and Society, not combat.
- **Ancestry:** dwarf. Darkvision.
- **Age:** about 400. Player Core gives a typical dwarven lifespan of around 350, so she is past it
  and living on borrowed time.
- **Then (c. -1350 AR):** a surveyor on the dig that broke into the Thassilonian ruin.
- **Now:** retired clan archivist. She carries a satchel of maps that are still correct, though the
  ground no longer matches them. She hired the protagonist as guard and guide into the depths of
  Maza-Khad, where the Labyrinth is rumoured to be.

## Voice — Saelith's register on Kelda's mind

- **Register:** languid, theatrical, darkly funny, courtly. Long ornamented sentences, and much of
  her condescension is a joke against herself.
- **Underneath:** a surveyor's precision. The flourish always ends in a measurement.
- **Tic:** she calls the protagonist by the wrong name, the name of a human guide dead two centuries,
  and corrects herself late or not at all.
- **Sample lines:**
  - *"Do keep up, Haldren. Ah, not Haldren. Haldren has been dead since before your grandmother's
    grandmother. You have his posture. Forty-one feet to the next timber, if the timber is still
    where I left it."*
  - *"You will be dead before any of this matters to you, which makes you the only honest party I
    could possibly hire."*
  - *"A city fell. Everyone agreed what killed it. Agreement is a very comfortable place to stop
    thinking."*
