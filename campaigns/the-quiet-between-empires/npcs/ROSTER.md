## The Last Witness — sidekick, not yet built

**Chosen at intake as the campaign's GM-run sidekick.** An ancient dwarf or elf who was in the
tunnel when dwarven digging broke into the Thassilonian ruin, at the tail of the last great
interhuman war. One of the handful who knows the city's fall was not the war's doing.

- **One job:** *knowing.* Lore and Society, not combat. Fragile by design.
- **Why this one matters more than a sidekick usually does:** canon rows 9 and the entry in
  `gm-private/secrets.md` say the generation who witnessed the wars is mostly dead and the truth
  is dying with them. Choosing this sidekick makes that abstraction a person the player has to
  keep alive.
- **Not yet built.** Name, ancestry (dwarf or elf), and numbers are settled at character creation.

### How the numbers will be derived — stated in advance

`tools/pf2e.py` has **no creature-benchmark command**, so the GM Core creature-building tables are
not in this repo. The build will therefore either:

1. **Reskin a published NPC** with numbers unchanged, cited by name, source and level — e.g.
   `Homebrew — reskin of <NPC> (NPC Core, lvl N)`; or
2. **Use the GM Core creature-building benchmarks read from Archives of Nethys**, with the table
   and URL cited in this file.

Numbers will not be freehanded. Charter rule 4.

### A GM obligation this choice creates

If the Last Witness dies, the player must not lose access to the truth entirely — that would turn
a bad round into a dead campaign. The answer is **not** plot armour: the witness takes damage and
can die like anyone. Instead, **every fact they hold has at least one other route to it** — another
survivor, a dwarven record, something carved where it should not be. Their death is then a real
loss and a real setback, not an ending. Recorded here so it is a standing obligation rather than
an improvisation under pressure.
