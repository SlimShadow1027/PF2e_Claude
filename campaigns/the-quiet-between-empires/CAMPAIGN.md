## Premise and stakes

**The Returning Road.**

- **What is wrong with the world:** A buried Thassilonian road network is surfacing under the
  hills on its own. Roads that were under forty feet of soil are at grade again, they are intact,
  and they go somewhere.
- **Who is causing it:** Nobody. There is no will behind it — a system is resuming. This is the
  "magic returning" setting assumption taken literally, and it means the campaign has no
  antagonist to defeat at the centre of it, only people reacting to it.
- **What happens if nobody stops it:** The network completes and distance stops being a defence.
  Things that were kept a month apart are a day's walk from anyone's door. The scattered holdings
  of this era survive on being hard to reach; the roads end that.

## Protagonist framing

Why is this character the one who acts?

**Obligation.** A post, a charge or an oath — a waystation, a watch, a toll, or a named stretch of
road. The surfacing network is this character's problem whether they want it or not, which gives
them standing to act and removes any need to be dragged from scene to scene in a sandbox. The
specific post is settled at character creation, not here.

## Setting assumptions kept

| Assumption | Decision |
|---|---|
| Gods and religion | The Golarion pantheon as published, but organised faith is sparse on this coast at this date — shrines and local practice, not cathedrals and hierarchies. |
| Planes and afterlife | The published cosmology, as written. |
| Ancestries present | **Dwarves and elves outnumber humans**, and noticeably so — this is their era on this coast, not humanity's. Humans are present but a minority. Halflings exist as a **small scattering of settlements**. **Gnomes do not exist**: they have not yet been cast out of the fey realm. **Goblins are present but primitive** — more savage and feral than their later-era presentation. Flavour only: any goblin stat block still reskins a published base with numbers unchanged. |
| Magic prevalence | returning — something that had gone quiet is coming back |
| Technology level | Pre-industrial on the surface — iron, timber, animal power, no clockwork and no firearms — with working Thassilonian ruin-tech recoverable from the deep sites. |

## Themes to keep returning to

- **Distance is the only defence anyone here has**, and it is being taken away by something
  that does not know it is doing it.
- **A road is not neutral.** It carries whatever uses it, in both directions, and nobody gets
  to choose which.
- **No one to blame.** The premise has no antagonist at its centre, so every antagonist the
  campaign produces is somebody reacting — reasonably, and badly.
- **The long-lived remember; the short-lived must act.** Dwarves and elves outnumber humans
  here, and someone alive saw the last time this happened.
- **Thassilon outlasted its own intent.** What is surfacing was built for a purpose nobody
  now living shares.
