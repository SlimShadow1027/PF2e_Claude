# Vessa Tarn

**GM-run ally, built as a full PC.** She is in the initiative order and levels alongside
Karsa. She does not take the spotlight, does not solve the scene, and never makes the
decision — she carries a build, a voice, and a reason to be there.

- **Player:** GM
- **Kind:** ally (full PC)
- **Level:** 1
- **Ancestry / heritage:** Human (**Skilled Heritage** — Deception) — *Player Core*
- **Background:** **Sailor** — *Player Core* p.88
- **Class:** **Ranger**, hunter's edge **Precision** — *Player Core* p.154
- **Key ability:** Dexterity
- **Languages:** Common, Varisian
- **Deity / philosophy:** none she would admit to. Salvage divers are superstitious rather
  than devout, and she is superstitious about this cliff in particular.

## Why she is built this way

She exists to close the gaps Karsa leaves, and the gaps are specific:

| Karsa's gap | What Vessa brings |
|---|---|
| Perception only **+6**, trained | **+7, expert** — and she takes the Scout exploration activity |
| No ranged attack at all; Flood Stance restricts Strikes to flooded river | **Shortbow +7**, 60-foot range, reload 0 |
| No Thievery, Crafting, Society, Nature or Deception | all five, trained |
| Nobody to **Administer First Aid** if Karsa goes down | she can, and **Cooperative Nature** makes her Aid checks +4 |

She is deliberately **not** dual-class and **not** mythic. Ancestry Paragon applies to her
because it is a campaign-wide variant, but the two variants that make Karsa exceptional are
Karsa's alone. This keeps her a genuine second body in the initiative order without making
the encounter math a two-protagonist problem. See `RULES_DELTAS.md`.

## Attacks

| Attack | Bonus | Damage | Traits / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shortbow** | **+7** | 1d6 piercing, **deadly d10** | range 60 ft, reload 0, Bulk 1, hands 1+ |
| **Shortsword** | **+7** | 1d6+1 piercing | agile, finesse, versatile S — finesse lets her use Dex to attack, Str still adds to damage |

- **Precision** (hunter's edge): the **first time each round** she hits her hunted prey, she
  deals **+1d8 precision damage**. Ineffective against anything without vital organs — which
  in a crypt is a real caveat, not a footnote.
- **Hunted Shot** (◆, once per round): **two shortbow Strikes** against her prey, MAP applied
  normally. Reload 0 is the requirement, which the shortbow meets.
- **Hunt Prey** (◆): +2 circumstance to Seek and to Track that creature, and it ignores the
  second-range-increment penalty against it.

**The combination worth naming:** flooded river has the **trip** trait, and Karsa has Titan
Wrestler. Karsa knocks something prone or grapples it — **off-guard, −2 AC** — and Vessa's
Hunted Shot lands two arrows into it with precision damage on the first. That is the party's
whole damage plan, and it runs through Karsa setting it up.

**Cooperative Nature is the most important feat on this sheet.** Aid is the action a
two-person party lives on, the typical DC is 15, and she Aids at +4 circumstance on top of the
relevant skill. A success grants Karsa +1; a critical success grants +2. She has to **prepare
to help on a previous turn** for it to trigger — that is the cost, and the GM must honour it.

## Backstory

Vessa Tarn dives the Cove for salvage and has done since she was twelve. She is not from
anywhere else; there is no interesting reason she is here. That is the point of her.

Thirty years of dredging under that cliff has produced rigging, coin, ballast, three
skeletons, and a great deal of nothing. Vessa found the door in the second week of Kuthona,
forty feet down, and her first instinct was not wonder. It was that a freestanding door
seated in bedrock is the single most dangerous thing she has ever seen underwater, because
somebody built it, and they built it *there*, and they built it to be **shut**.

She reported it because the bounty is the bounty. Then the Cove built a windlass over it and
started charging people to look, and Vessa has spent the six weeks since trying and failing
to explain to anyone in town why that is not a good idea.

She is the only person who has been down there and come back. She will tell Karsa everything
she remembers, and some of what she remembers is wrong.
