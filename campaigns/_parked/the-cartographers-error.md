# The Cartographer's Error

**Parked 2026-09-30**, at the player's request, after the intake for *Third Beginnings*.
Their words: *"Keep the second idea as the next campaign choice, I really liked that
premise."* This is a pitch, not a campaign. Nothing here has been established as canon
anywhere.

- **Setting:** Golarion as published — the Mushfens, south of Sandpoint
- **Genre:** exploration/hexcrawl, with a dungeon that is the same dungeon every time
- **Levels:** built as a 1 to 5-8 campaign, same as *Third Beginnings*
- **Framing:** curiosity, souring into obligation

## The hook

The Sandpoint Cartographic Society has surveyed the Mushfens for two hundred years. Every map
disagrees about a seventeen-square-mile region on the Soggy River, and every surveyor sent to
settle it has come back with a *different* answer, drawn confidently, all of them mutually
exclusive. The Society has stopped sending people. The protagonist goes because nobody else
is asking the obvious question: what if all the maps are right?

## The place

A true hexcrawl through swamp that does not hold still, punctuated by ruins that appear in
some crossings and not others. The dungeon is the same dungeon every time, entered from a
different century.

## The curse

The protagonist starts keeping their own map. By chapter two theirs is the only one that
works — and by chapter three the region has begun conforming to it. Everything they draw
becomes true. Including the mistakes.

## The ally

**Odo Fennick**, a Society surveyor who came back wrong eleven years ago and was pensioned
off. Wizard or investigator. He remembers four separate versions of the same expedition and
cannot tell which happened.

## The moral choice

There are people living in the contested hexes. They exist in some versions and not others.
Fixing the map means deciding which of them are real.

## Notes for whoever builds this

- This is the slowest burn of the three pitches and the hardest to run in short sittings. It
  wants an evening at a time, which suits the preferences recorded for this player.
- The hexcrawl has to genuinely matter or the premise collapses into a gimmick. Budget real
  travel time, real supply pressure, and real consequences for crossing the same hex twice.
- The "everything you draw becomes true" mechanic needs a hard rule written before play, or
  it becomes an argument. Decide in intake whether it is a resource, a clock, or a curse
  with a fixed trigger.
- If *Third Beginnings* ends with Varisia meaningfully changed, consider running this one in
  a shared world under `worlds/` so the two campaigns can share a Varisia with a date gate
  between them. That is exactly what `system/21-shared-worlds.md` is for.
