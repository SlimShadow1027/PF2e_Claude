# Timeline — The Candle Road

The in-world calendar and what happened when. One row per notable day. Advance the clock with
`python3 tools/state.py --campaign candle-road advance-time "4 hours"` — this file is
the human-readable record of where that clock has been.

- **Calendar:** generic — SRD 5.2 publishes no calendar; name the setting's in intake, or define one in the world's CALENDAR.md
- **Campaign began:** 1 Month 1 1
- **Current in-world date:** see `CHECKPOINT.md` (rendered from `state.json`)
