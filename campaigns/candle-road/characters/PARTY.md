# Party at a glance — The Candle Road

The numbers here are a convenience copy. **`state.json` is canonical** for everything
volatile — current HP, conditions, resources. When they disagree, `state.json` wins; run
`python3 tools/state.py --campaign candle-road render` and read `CHECKPOINT.md`.

**Keep the table for this campaign's ruleset and delete the other.** `System:` is in
`CAMPAIGN.md`; `python3 tools/rules.py which candle-road`.

## Reactions available

The GM checks this list before resolving any trigger. Forgetting to offer a reaction is the
most common way an automated GM quietly shortchanges a player.

| Character | Reaction | Trigger | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

In Pathfinder the usual suspects are Reactive Strike and Shield Block; in D&D 2024 they are the
Opportunity Attack, Shield, Counterspell, Protection and anything readied. **A readied action's
trigger goes in this table when it is readied**, so "that is not what you readied" is backable.
